Delete Your PocketPass Account

You can request deletion of your PocketPass account and associated personal data using the Gmail address linked to your Google Sign-In account.

What data will be deleted?

  • Your PocketPass account and registered Gmail address.
  • Your name and phone number.
  • Your selected state and district information.
  • Your profile picture and associated stored image.
  • Other personal data associated with your account, where applicable.
Important: Permanent Deletion

Once your account and associated data have been deleted, they cannot be recovered. Your Google account itself will not be deleted.

How to request account deletion?

Click the button below to open your email application. Send the request from your registered Gmail address so we can identify your PocketPass account.

Request Account Deletion

Deletion requests are intended to be processed within 30 days after verification. Keep this statement only if your actual deletion process meets this timeline.
