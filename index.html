Delete Your PocketPass Account

If you want to delete your PocketPass account, send us an account deletion request using the Gmail address linked to your Google Sign-In account.

What data will be deleted?

  • Your account and registered Gmail address.
  • Your name and phone number.
  • Your selected state and district information.
  • Your profile picture and associated stored image.
  • Other personal data associated with your account, where applicable.

Important: Account deletion is permanent. Once your account and data have been deleted, they cannot be recovered. Your Google account will not be deleted.

How to request deletion?

Click the button below and send an email from your registered Gmail address. Include your registered Gmail address in the message so we can identify your account.

Request Account Deletion

Deletion requests will be processed within 30 days after verification, provided this timeline matches our actual deletion process.
