Delete Your PocketPass Account

You can request deletion of your PocketPass account by sending an email from your registered Google Sign-In Gmail address.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Your account and registered email address
  • Your name and phone number
  • Your state and district information
  • Your profile picture and stored image
  • Other associated personal data, where applicable

How to Delete Your Account

Click the button below and send an account deletion request using your registered Gmail address.

Request Account Deletion

Important

Account deletion is permanent. Once your account and associated data have been deleted, they cannot be recovered. Your Google account will not be deleted.

Verified deletion requests will be processed within 30 days, provided this matches our actual deletion process.
