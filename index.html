Account Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your PocketPass account and associated personal data. This page explains how to submit your request, what data may be deleted, and what to expect.

1. How to Delete Your PocketPass Account

You can request deletion of your PocketPass account using either of the following methods.

Option A: Through the PocketPass app

  1. Open the PocketPass app on your device.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Open your Profile screen.
  4. Select the Delete Account option.
  5. Read the warning carefully and confirm your request if you want to proceed.

If you cannot access the app, you may use the email method below.

Option B: Request deletion by email

Send an account deletion request from the email address associated with your PocketPass account. Include your registered email address and state that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.

Request Account Deletion
Important: The email button opens your email application. It does not automatically delete your account. Your request must be received and processed by PocketPass support.

2. What Data Will Be Deleted?

Once your deletion request has been verified and processed, PocketPass will delete the following data associated with your account, where applicable:

  • Your PocketPass account and associated authentication identity.
  • Your profile information, such as your name, phone number, email address, state and district.
  • Your uploaded profile photograph and associated stored image.
  • Personal records associated with your account that are maintained by PocketPass.

Account deletion will also include associated personal data stored in PocketPass systems, subject to applicable legal requirements and any valid retention obligations.

3. What Data Is Not Deleted?

PocketPass may contain educational materials such as books, PDFs, practice questions and other learning resources provided for users. These are shared educational resources, not your personal account data, and are not deleted when an individual account is removed.

Information that must be retained to comply with applicable law or address legitimate security and legal obligations may be retained only as required and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

4. How Long Does Deletion Take?

PocketPass aims to process verified account deletion requests within 30 days of receipt.

If additional verification is required or exceptional circumstances affect processing, support may contact you using the email address associated with your request.

Any data subject to a valid legal retention requirement will be handled according to the applicable requirement.

5. Important Consequences of Deletion

Warning: Account deletion is permanent.

After your account has been deleted, you may no longer be able to sign in to your existing account or access data associated with it. Your deleted personal information cannot be restored through PocketPass.

  • Account-related information may be permanently removed.
  • You may lose access to your existing account and profile.
  • If you use PocketPass again, you may need to create a new account.
  • Deleting the app from your phone alone does not delete your PocketPass account.

6. Account Deletion and Google Sign-In

If you used Google Sign-In to access PocketPass, deleting your PocketPass account removes your PocketPass account and associated data as described above. This does not automatically delete your Google account.

PocketPass account deletion and Google account deletion are separate processes.

7. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I recover my deleted account?

No. Once account deletion is completed, your deleted account information cannot be restored.

Will uninstalling PocketPass delete my account?

No. You must use the in-app deletion option or submit a deletion request using the contact method provided on this page.

Can I request deletion without accessing the app?

Yes. Contact PocketPass support from your registered email address and request account deletion.

Will my Google account also be deleted?

No. Only your PocketPass account is covered by this request.

How can I check my deletion request?

Contact PocketPass support using the email address listed below. Include your request details so the team can help verify its status.
